Privacy Policy — Tidy Tens

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Contact: tidytens.app@gmail.com

Published by: Elise Reppond

The short version

Tidy Tens has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Everything stays on your phone, with one exception: when you clear the daily board, that run is sent to the daily leaderboard, under an anonymous id and the name you chose, so it can be ranked against other players' runs. See "The daily leaderboard" below.

What the app stores

Everything the app saves stays in its own private storage on your device. Nothing is transmitted. Specifically, it keeps:

  • how many stars you have earned on each stage
  • your coin balance
  • how many hints, undos and shuffles you own
  • which stages you have unlocked, and which board you are playing on each one
  • which one-time explanatory pop-ups you have already seen
  • your daily streak: the days you cleared the daily board, any missed day you restored, and which streak rewards you have been given
  • your phone's time-zone offset from UTC (for example "+5:30") when you last cleared the daily board, so a date skipped by flying east does not break your streak. It is a number of minutes, not a place, and like everything here it never leaves the phone
  • whether you turned on the daily reminder
  • whether developer/testing mode is switched on, which is a device-only setting

None of that identifies you. There is no email, no account, no contact information, no location, no device identifier, no advertising identifier, and no usage or diagnostic reporting. The name you type is only on your phone, unless you clear a daily board (below).

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask you to sign in. The leaderboard uses an anonymous id made for your install, never an account.
  • It does not show advertising.
  • It does not include analytics, crash reporting, or any tracking of any kind.
  • It does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • It does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.
  • It asks permission to show notifications only if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is set and shown by your phone itself; nothing is sent anywhere to make it happen, and you can turn it off in the app's Settings or your phone's.
  • It makes no network requests except to send a cleared daily board to the leaderboard and to show the day's top runs on the Daily screen.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores on your phone goes when you delete the app. Runs you sent to the daily leaderboard are deleted with Settings → "Delete my leaderboard data": do that before deleting the app, because afterwards nothing ties those runs to you any more. You can also write to the contact address above.

Children

The app asks for no age and no personal details. The only thing other players can see is the name and scores on the daily leaderboard; names are filtered (rude words, links, @handles and official-looking names show as "Player"), and the publisher removes names that are reported. It contains no advertising, no links out, and no way to message anyone.

Apple and Google

Apple, or Google on Android, may collect its own information about downloads and purchases, under its own privacy policy rather than this one. That is between you and the store; the app does not receive it.

The daily leaderboard

When you clear the daily board, the app sends that run to a server so it can be listed against other players' runs. Nothing else is sent: the chapters, the modes and your progress stay on your phone. Settings has a "Delete my leaderboard data" button.

What is sent, and only then:

  • an anonymous id made for your install (a random string; not your name, email, phone number, Apple ID or device identifier)
  • the name you typed in Settings, if any; otherwise "Player". A name the filter refuses (rude words, links, @handles, names that look official) is listed as "Player", and Settings tells you so as you type it
  • the day's board number, your score, how many adds you used, how long you took, and the list of moves you made, which the server replays to check the score is real

Where it goes: a Firebase project (Google Cloud) run by the publisher, in the United States (nam5). Firebase's own terms and privacy policy apply to the hosting; the publisher does not use Firebase Analytics, advertising features or any other Google service with this data.

What it keeps from it: your best run of the day (name, score, adds, time and its moves), how many clean runs of the board you cleared that day, and a short code worked out from each counted run's moves and time, so a run sent twice is counted once.

What it is used for: ranking the day's board and showing it in the app. Nothing else: no profiling, no advertising, no sharing with anyone.

What other players see: your listed name, score, adds and time, and how many times you cleared the board that day ("3 clears"), on the day's board. Never the id, never the moves.

Deleting it: Settings → "Delete my leaderboard data" removes every run you have sent, at once. Deleting the app also abandons the anonymous id, so nothing sent later could be tied to you, but the runs already sent stay on the board until you delete them from within the app first.

App Privacy answers for a version with the leaderboard on: Data collected, linked to you: User ID (the anonymous id) and Gameplay Content (name, scores, moves), for App Functionality; not used for tracking. Everything else stays "Not collected".

Changes to this policy

Two changes are planned, and neither has shipped:

  • Cloud saves, so progress survives a new phone. That would mean storing the same game progress listed above on a server.
  • Advertising, which would involve a third-party advertising provider with its own data practices.

If either arrives, this policy will be updated and the change described before the version that includes it is released. Nothing in the app today does either.
